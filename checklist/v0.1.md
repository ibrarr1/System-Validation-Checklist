## AOUT design check
- **Atomic:** Each row asks one checkable question.
- **Observable:** Each answer can be determined by inspecting the supplied SRS.
- **Unambiguous:** Questions refer to specific requirements or named clinic risks/stakeholders.
- **Traceable:** Each item identifies its source.

## Notes
- A dash (—) means this checklist template has not itself been executed. Completed assessments are in `runs/run-A.md` and `runs/run-B.md`.
- The checklist contains 18 items and covers all five stakeholder roles and more than five clinic risks.
